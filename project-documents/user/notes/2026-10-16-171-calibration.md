### Why the dollar floor

At M=12 with no floor, the universe week marked 52,817 bars, and 21,945 of
them (42%) fell in regular hours. On 2026-10-08 the regular-hours mark rate by
symbol liquidity (fifths by regular-hours dollar volume) was 2.3%, 1.2%,
0.56%, 0.19% and 0.04%, least to most liquid. Sampled marks were real moves:

- ARRY 15:34 ET, low 3.750: the bottom of a seven-bar one-tick dip. Closes
  barely move, so the median close change is 0 and the threshold fell to
  `R × close`, about two ticks. Now fixture `ARRY_2026-10-08.csv`.
- DBC, EWQ, PLSE, VSGX all at 12:17 ET: one market-wide move, marked in each.

Regular-hours marks on 2026-10-08 by floor: none 4,470; $0.02 1,440;
$0.03 1,311; $0.05 1,207. With the $0.02 floor the rates by liquidity fifth
became 1.5%, 0.22%, 0.08%, 0.06% and 0.04%. The least liquid fifth (median 5
regular-hours bars a day) stays high: ten neighbouring bars span hours there.

## Insert failure path

Traced in `data/acquisition/daemon/minute.py` (task 4.2). `_insert_minute_bars`
runs inside the chunk transaction of `_do_minute_symbol`, together with
`_advance_minute_gap` and `_record_minute_attempt`. When marking raises,
`mark_inserted` logs with `logger.exception` and re-raises. The chunk
transaction then rolls back: the bars, the gap-row advance and the attempt
record. The exception leaves `_do_minute_symbol` and reaches
`_process_minute_symbol`'s last-resort handler. That handler logs it and
returns a `MinuteFailureKind.DATABASE` failure, the same as any database
error on that insert. The symbol's later chunks in that pass are not
attempted. Earlier chunks committed with their bars marked, and the failed
chunk's gap row is unchanged, so the next pass retries it. No unevaluated row
is committed, which matches design failure-table row 1.

`write_minute_data_bulk`: marking runs before `conn.commit()`. A raise rolls
the connection back and the existing handler returns `False`.

## Sweep: one production-sized chunk (task 6.5)

`uv run python scripts/measure_off_tape_sweep_chunk.py --start
2026-10-01T00:00Z --end 2026-10-08T00:00Z`: production's chunk for
2026-10-01 to 10-08 (10,074,248 rows, copied read-only in 59 s) into a
throwaway database on the test cluster, compressed, then `sweep_chunk`:

- 10,074,248 rows in 399.2 s = 25,237 rows/s; 29,937 marked (0.297%).
- Projection for all 4,552,244,737 rows (`approximate_row_count`, 2026-10-16):
  about 50 h, single process. Weekly minute passes are 7 days apart, so no
  workers are needed.
- An insert into a chunk the sweep holds waits for at most one chunk rewrite
  (about 400 s at this size); design failure-table row 4.
- Side effect: each chunk's `UPDATE` logs a cagg invalidation for its range.
  The minute caggs' refresh policies look back 10 days, so older entries stay
  in the log; values are unchanged (`off_tape` is in no cagg), and a later
  wide manual refresh re-materializes those ranges at its normal cost.
