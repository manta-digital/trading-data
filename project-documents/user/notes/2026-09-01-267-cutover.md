## Handoff — the descent to the floor (not a task)

From `2026-06-29T02:00Z` to the floor is ~4,300 hours of tape; at ~30–45
walked hours per firing that is a few days of hourly firings for the dense
July tail, accelerating as the tape thins (the rehearsal measured
39–213 pages/hour for Jan–Jun). `behind cutoff, uncollected` (~585 k after
the walk) drains at 1,000/firing until `floor reached`, then takes the whole
cap. Watch `mt-run data kalshi status`: the historical line's range grows
downward every firing; a watermark that stops moving without `floor
reached` is the signal to act on (runbook 100, *Kalshi*).
